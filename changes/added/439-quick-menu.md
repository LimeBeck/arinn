- Quick menu: `quick_menu_shortcut` (default Meta+F11) in `config.toml` opens a side sheet over the running application with Now playing and play/pause, the sound and network state, Sleep and Power, Open Home and Close Arinn. Back returns to the same application. On KDE the daemon writes the key to the new KWin action "Arinn: Quick menu"; the X11/EWMH adapter grabs it next to the Home keys ([ADR-0106](docs/adr/0106-quick-menu-presentation.md), [#439](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/439)).
