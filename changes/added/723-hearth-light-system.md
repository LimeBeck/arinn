- Home, Applications, the Library and a title's detail are lit from below: a dark ceiling, a warm floor and a pool of light under the focused item. The pool takes the artwork's hue, or the item's identity hue, and follows focus. In the evening and at night the light dims, and a warm veil covers those screens and also Settings and Downloads. The player stays uncovered. Settings › Appearance › Light follows the time of day turns that off; Light field still turns the floor and the pool off ([ADR-0136](docs/adr/0136-hearth-light-visual-direction.md)).
