- Home, Applications, the Library and a title's detail are lit from below: a dark ceiling, a warm floor and a pool of light under the focused item. The pool takes the artwork's hue, or the item's identity hue, and follows focus. In the evening and at night the light dims and a warm veil covers those screens. At night the text is raised, and in the evening the hints are raised, so the weakest text stays at or above 4.5:1 on the brightest point of that light. Settings › Appearance › Light follows the time of day turns that off; Light field still turns the floor and the pool off ([ADR-0136](docs/adr/0136-hearth-light-visual-direction.md)).
