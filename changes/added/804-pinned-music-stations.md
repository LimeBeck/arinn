- Home's Favourites can now hold pinned music stations. A pin keeps its name while its music source is away and is marked unavailable, with the reason, while the source is not connected or no longer knows the station. Selecting a pin starts the station with the usual queue replacement question, and unpinning works from the shelf item's actions ([#804](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/804), [ADR-0142](docs/adr/0142-music-in-hearth-light.md) MH-6). Pinning from the player's *More* sheet follows with #802.
