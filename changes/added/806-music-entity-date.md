- Music entries can now carry the date they joined the library (optional `added_at` in the [music protocol](docs/plugin-api/music.md)). Local music reports when each file was first found, and albums and artists take their newest track's date. This prepares the date order of the shared music shelves ([ADR-0142](docs/adr/0142-music-in-hearth-light.md), [#806](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/806)).
