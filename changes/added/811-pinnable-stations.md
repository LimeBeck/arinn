- Music stations can now be marked `pinnable` in the [music protocol](docs/plugin-api/music.md): such a station keeps its ID across provider restarts and never reuses it, so Arinn can store it and start it later ([ADR-0142](docs/adr/0142-music-in-hearth-light.md) MH-6, [#811](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/811)). Yandex stations carry the mark. Local music gains its own station, "All my music, shuffled", which plays the library shuffled and keeps topping up its queue; starting radio from a local track or album plays it first.
