- The quick menu is part of the MVP: a second global key (`quick_menu_shortcut`) opens a side sheet over the running application with Now playing, volume and mute, network status, Sleep and the power menu, Open Home and Close Arinn. Back returns to the same application; media and volume keys outside the shell stay with Plasma (spec §5.7, §6.2, §6.3, [#439](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/439)).
