- The shell uses the Hearth light palette and typography ([ADR-0136](docs/adr/0136-hearth-light-visual-direction.md), [#722](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/722)): Charcoal with warm text, Ysabeau for content titles, sentence-case eyebrows, and page titles at weight 600. A long title steps down and wraps instead of being cut to one line. The hero reserves two lines at 68 px and the title grows upward in that slot, so the rows stay put and the meta keeps a 60 px gap. At night the weakest text is held to `textContrastMinimum` (4.8) on the veiled background, a plate, the night focused plate, Home, Applications, and Settings and Downloads. The focused plate uses a darker fill at night so the text stays at the light-field minimum.
