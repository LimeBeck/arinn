- The shell uses the Hearth light palette and typography ([ADR-0136](docs/adr/0136-hearth-light-visual-direction.md), [#722](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/722)): Charcoal with warm text, Ysabeau for content titles, sentence-case eyebrows, and page titles at weight 600. A long title steps down and wraps instead of being cut to one line. The hero reserves two lines at 68 px and the title grows upward in that slot, so the rows stay put and the meta keeps a 60 px gap. At night the weakest text is held to `textContrastMinimum` (4.8) on the veiled background, a plate, the night focused plate, Home, Applications, and Settings and Downloads. The focused plate uses a darker fill at night so the text stays at the light-field minimum. Settings focus plates use that same fill, and a screen that hardcodes a white focus plate at 20% fails the focus-fill check. A fitted title keeps its size and line count as properties the text bindings follow, so a later text or font change is measured again. Evening contrast uses `eveningContrastMinimum` (4.5), next to the night bar: evening Home is about 4.50:1 and a focused plate about 4.78:1, both below 4.8, and the owner has not chosen the evening threshold.
