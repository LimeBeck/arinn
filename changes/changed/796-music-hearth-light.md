- The music design is now part of Hearth light. Music becomes a Home-like room with shelves and a calmer *Now playing*. While it plays, music shows in the status area, as the first Continue card, as pinned stations in Favourites, as a media card in the Quick menu and on Ambient. Automatic Ambient may now start while music plays. See the [music Hearth light brief](docs/design/briefs/music-hearth-light/README.md) and [ADR-0142](docs/adr/0142-music-in-hearth-light.md) ([#796](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/796)).
