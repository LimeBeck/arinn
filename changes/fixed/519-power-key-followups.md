- X11/EWMH: when Arinn lets go of the power key and its repair of xfce4-power-manager's grab cannot be confirmed (the grab check fails, the restart fails or the new instance does not appear in time), the running adapter now tries again at most every 30 s, so xfce4-power-manager gets the key back within about 30 s instead of only at the adapter's next start. A slowly starting xfce4-power-manager is waited for, and after three unconfirmed restarts in a row Arinn stops restarting it and leaves the key to the desktop. An xfce4-power-manager the user stopped is never restarted. The XFCE login restore of the power button setting holds a temporary logind power-key inhibitor, and retries of a stale setting open the system bus again when it was not available at the adapter's start ([#519](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/519)).
