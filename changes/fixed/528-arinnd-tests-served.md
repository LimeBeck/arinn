- More `arinnd` private-bus tests now wait until the object they call is served. These are the media association, browse, settings, download and Activity background tests. Like the system-control tests in #524, they called a connection right after its first export with no round trip in between, so the first call could be dropped and the test could hang. The `served` and `.bounded()` helpers now live in one shared test-support module ([#528](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/528)).
