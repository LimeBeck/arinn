# Showcase screenshots

These PNGs are the README, website and handbook screenshots. They are the
captures made on 2026-10-08 (`keyboard.png` on 2026-10-10) with Qt 6.11.2/6.12.0
by the former screen gallery, which #567 removed from `arinn-ui-gallery`.
They are kept unchanged until they are re-shot from `tests/ui/visual` on
Qt 6.10 or later ([#569](https://github.com/LimeBeck/arinn/issues/569)).
The current fixture renders and their provenance are in
[current-ui](../current-ui/README.md).
